Gearing Info

Gear Ratio: Chainring teeth ÷ Cog teeth

Gear Inches: (Chainring ÷ Cog) × Wheel diameter in inches

Gain Ratio: (Chainring ÷ Cog) × (Wheel radius ÷ Crank length)

Speed: Gear inches × π × Cadence × 0.001885

Lower gear inches = easier climbing, Higher gear inches = faster on flats
