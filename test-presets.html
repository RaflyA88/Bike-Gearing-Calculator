🚴 Fixie Gearing Notes:

  • 46×16 (Standard): Balanced ratio for mixed terrain
  • 48×17 (Fast): Higher top speed, harder to accelerate
  • 44×18 (Easy): Easier for hills and stop-and-go traffic
  • Fixies use single-speed gearing (one chainring, one cog)
  • Gear inches typically range from 70-80 for comfortable riding

🏙️ City Bike Gearing Notes:

  • 44×16 (26" wheels): Good for hilly cities
  • 46×18 (700c wheels): Balanced for flat to rolling terrain
  • 38×18 (Comfort): Easy pedaling for casual riding
  • City bikes prioritize comfort over speed
  • Wider tires (35-40mm) for better comfort and stability
